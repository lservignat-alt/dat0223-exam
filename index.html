Minimum Spanning Trees & Kruskal 10 pts

This exercise covers the theory and practice of minimum spanning trees (MST) and their construction using Kruskal's algorithm.

Open-ended questions — written answers only.

Theory

Q2 — Kruskal's Algorithm Steps (2 pts)
Describe step by step how Kruskal's algorithm builds a minimum spanning tree.
Q3 — Role of Union-Find (3 pts)
Explain the role of the Union-Find data structure in Kruskal's algorithm. Why is it necessary? What problem does it solve?
Q4 — Time Complexity (2 pts)

Q5 — Kruskal vs Prim (1 pt)
